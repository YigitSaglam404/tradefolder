Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Your trades are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud account. We cannot see your data.

My trades are not syncing between devices.
Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud is turned on for TradeFolder in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I delete all my data?
Open TradeFolder, go to Settings and choose Delete All Trades. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.

Do I need an account?
No. TradeFolder works without sign-up or login.

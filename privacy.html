Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

TradeFolder is a trading journal for iPhone. We built it so that your trading data stays yours. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We do not collect your data

TradeFolder does not have accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We, the developers, do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or trading data.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. The trades you enter and your app settings are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud is turned on, your trades are synced through your own private iCloud database, managed by Apple. We have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on the app lock, TradeFolder asks iOS to verify your identity. Biometric data never leaves the secure hardware of your device and is never shared with the app or with us.

Exchange rates

To show amounts in your chosen currency, the app downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. These requests contain no personal or trading data. As with any internet request, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address.

Deleting your data

You can delete all trades from the app's Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can remove the iCloud copy in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Children

TradeFolder is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
